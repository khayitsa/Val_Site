Jewellery Care

{% include 'legal/_draft_banner.html' %}

Your Verayah piece is handmade from 18k gold-coated chains, river pearls and natural crystals. It is water resistant, not waterproof. A little care keeps it vibrant and helps it resist tarnish over time.

  • Take it off before swimming, showering, exercising and sleeping.
  • Put it on after perfume, lotion and sunscreen, not before.
  • Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing.
  • Store it dry, in its pouch or box, away from other jewellery so chains do not tangle or scratch.
  • Keep pearls and crystals away from harsh chemicals, heat and knocks.
