Cookie Policy

1. Introduction

Verayah ("we", "us", "our" or "the Business") uses cookies and similar technologies on its website to support website functionality, improve the browsing experience and understand how visitors interact with our jewellery collections and online store.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, why we use them and how you can manage your cookie preferences.

This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, mobile phone or other electronic device when you visit a website.

They help websites recognise your device, remember certain preferences and collect information about how visitors use the website.

Some cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly, while others help improve website performance, understand visitor behaviour or support marketing activities.

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

Depending on the website functionality and services implemented, Verayah may use the following categories of cookies.

3.1 Essential Cookies

Essential cookies support the basic operation and security of our website.

They may be used to maintain website sessions, support navigation, remember necessary settings and ensure that essential website functions operate correctly.

These cookies are necessary for the proper functioning of the website.

3.2 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow our website to remember certain preferences and settings to improve your browsing experience.

For example, they may help remember your selected preferences or other information that makes it easier to navigate our website.

3.3 Analytical and Performance Cookies

Analytical cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

They may collect information about pages visited, time spent on the website, browsing patterns, device types and general visitor activity.

We may use this information to improve our website, understand customer interests and identify areas where the browsing experience can be improved.

3.4 Marketing Cookies

Where implemented, marketing cookies may be used to understand the effectiveness of promotional activities and display relevant advertisements or promotional content.

These cookies may be provided by third-party platforms and may track interactions with our website and other online services.

Non-essential marketing cookies will only be used where the appropriate consent has been obtained.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Certain features on our website may rely on third-party services that place their own cookies on your device.

These may include website analytics services, social media integrations, embedded content providers and other external technology services.

Such third parties may collect information about your interaction with their services in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies.

Verayah does not control the independent cookie practices of third-party providers.

5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Where a cookie preference tool is available on our website, you may use it to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies or select the categories of cookies you wish to allow.

You may also manage or delete cookies through your internet browser settings.

Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete existing cookies or receive notifications before cookies are stored on your device.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of some parts of our website.

6. How Long Cookies Remain on Your Device

Some cookies are temporary and are automatically deleted when you close your browser. These are known as session cookies.

Other cookies remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them manually. These are known as persistent cookies.

The duration of a cookie depends on its purpose and the settings of the relevant website or third-party service.

7. Personal Information and Cookies

Certain cookies may collect information that can identify or be associated with an individual.

Any personal information collected through cookies will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that information collected through cookies is used only for appropriate website, business and customer-related purposes.

8. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, technology, services or business practices.

Any updates will be published on our website together with a revised date.
