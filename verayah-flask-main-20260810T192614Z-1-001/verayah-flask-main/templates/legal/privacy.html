What we collect, and why

  • Orders: your name, email, phone/WhatsApp, delivery address and city, what you ordered, payment method and any note you add. We use this to prepare, deliver and support your order. You do not need an account to order.
  • Contact messages: your name, email and message, so we can reply.
  • Optional account: if you choose to create one, your name, email, a password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text), and any phone, address, wishlist or past orders you save. You can use the shop without one.
  • Newsletter / sneak-peek club: your email (and name if you give it), and which list(s) you chose. This is separate from buying or having an account, and always optional.
  • Optional extras such as birthdays or jewellery sizes: not decided — the site does not collect these yet.

Payments

We take payment by Yappy or bank transfer, arranged directly with you after you place an order. The website does not process or store card or bank details.

We only send newsletter or sneak-peek emails to people who choose them, and every email has an unsubscribe link. Marketing is never a condition of buying. WhatsApp or Instagram promotions: not decided. Customer reviews or photos are only used with your permission: process not decided.

Only the owner. Delivery details are shared with the courier (Uno Express) so your parcel can reach you. Where the website data is stored: hosting provider not decided.

Cookies

The site uses one essential cookie to keep your shopping bag and sign-in working, and no analytics or advertising cookies. See the Cookies page.

How long we keep it

Order records: retention period not decided. Newsletter details until you unsubscribe. Account details until you delete your account.

Your choices

You can update your details and delete your account yourself from My Account.
