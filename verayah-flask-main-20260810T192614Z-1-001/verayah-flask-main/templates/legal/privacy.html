Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Verayah ("we", "us", "our" or "the Business") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information when you visit our website, browse our jewellery collections, place an order, request a customised product or communicate with us.

We aim to handle your personal information responsibly and transparently and only use it for legitimate business purposes connected with our jewellery products and services.

By using our website or providing us with your personal information, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy.

Verayah is a handmade jewellery business based in Panama City, Republic of Panama. We specialise in handcrafted jewellery inspired by the ocean and natural surroundings, including jewellery incorporating pearls, crystals and selected metal components.

3. Personal Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when using our website, placing an order, requesting a customised jewellery piece or communicating with us.

This information may include your full name, email address, telephone number, delivery address and other contact details necessary to process and fulfil your order.

When you purchase jewellery from us, we may also collect information relating to your order, including the products selected, quantities, payment confirmations, transaction references, delivery arrangements and purchase history.

For customised jewellery orders, we may collect additional information such as preferred designs, measurements, material selections, personalisation requests, photographs and other specifications necessary to create your requested product.

We may also collect information contained in communications you send to us through email, WhatsApp, social media or our website.

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, access times and general website usage information.

We do not intentionally collect more personal information than is reasonably necessary for our business purposes.

4. How We Collect Your Information

We collect personal information directly from you when you browse our website, submit an enquiry, place an order, request customised jewellery, communicate with us or voluntarily provide feedback about our products.

We may also receive information through payment confirmations from Yappy or banking institutions, delivery and courier service providers, and other service providers involved in fulfilling your order.

Certain technical information may be collected automatically through cookies, website analytics tools and similar technologies.

5. How We Use Your Personal Information

We use your personal information to operate our business and provide our products and services.

This includes processing and confirming orders, preparing and delivering jewellery, communicating with customers, responding to enquiries, preparing invoices, verifying payments and managing customer accounts where applicable.

For customised jewellery, we use the information you provide to discuss designs, confirm specifications, obtain approvals and manufacture products according to your requirements.

We may also use your information to arrange deliveries, communicate tracking information, manage returns and exchanges, investigate complaints, process refunds and provide after-sales assistance.

In addition, we may use personal information to maintain business records, improve our website, understand customer preferences, prevent fraudulent transactions and protect the security of our business.

Where you have agreed to receive promotional communications, we may use your contact details to inform you about new collections, products, special offers and other Verayah updates.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time.

6. Payment Information

Verayah currently accepts payments through Yappy and bank transfers.

Payment arrangements and the relevant payment details are communicated directly to customers after an order has been submitted.

We may receive and retain payment confirmations, transaction references, payment dates and other information reasonably necessary to verify payments and maintain our business records.

We do not currently collect credit or debit card details directly through our website.

Customers should never provide their banking passwords, PINs or confidential banking credentials to us or to anyone claiming to represent Verayah.

7. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

However, we may share personal information with trusted third parties where reasonably necessary to operate our business and fulfil your requests.

These parties may include payment service providers, banks, courier companies such as Uno Express, website hosting providers, website administrators, technology service providers and professional advisers.

For example, we may provide your name, telephone number and delivery address to a courier company to facilitate delivery of your order.

We may also disclose personal information where necessary to protect our business, prevent fraud, resolve disputes or respond to a legitimate request from a competent authority.

Where we share personal information with service providers, we take reasonable steps to ensure that the information is handled securely and used only for the relevant purpose.

8. International Transfers of Personal Information

Some of our website hosting, communication, payment or technology service providers may operate outside Panama.

Where personal information is transferred or accessed internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect the information and maintain its confidentiality.

9. Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected.

Customer information may be retained for the period necessary to process orders, provide after-sales assistance, manage warranty claims, maintain transaction records, resolve disputes and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Information relating to customised jewellery may be retained where necessary to maintain design specifications, facilitate repeat orders or address customer queries.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete, destroy or anonymise it.

10. Protection of Personal Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Access to customer information is limited to individuals and service providers who reasonably require it to perform their responsibilities.

Although we take reasonable precautions to safeguard personal information, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers are encouraged to exercise caution when sharing personal or payment-related information through electronic communication channels.

11. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to request information about the personal information we hold about you.

Subject to appropriate verification and any legitimate reason for retaining the information, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate or incomplete information, deletion of information where appropriate, or restriction of certain processing activities.

You may also object to certain uses of your personal information, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent and request a copy of your information in an appropriate electronic format.

Where you have subscribed to marketing communications, you may unsubscribe at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact details provided in this Privacy Policy. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before responding to your request.

You may also contact the competent data protection authority in Panama if you have concerns regarding the handling of your personal information.

Verayah is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of independent third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business operations, website functionality or information-handling practices.

Any changes will be published on our website together with an updated revision date.

We encourage visitors to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
